Three steps to your first subtitle.

01 · PLAY

Open a video.

Start playback on a normal web page, then open Subtitle Overlay from your browser toolbar.

02 · CHOOSE

Bring your subtitles.

Search for subtitles or choose a supported subtitle file from your computer.

03 · WATCH

Settle in.

Position the overlay and adjust the timing if needed. Your video keeps playing underneath.
