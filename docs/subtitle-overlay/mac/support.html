Getting started.

  1. Open Safari Settings, choose Extensions, and switch on Subtitle Overlay. Opening the app from Applications takes you straight there.
  2. Open a page with a video, click Subtitle Overlay in Safari’s toolbar, and choose Always Allow on This Website. Safari extensions start with no access to any page, so without this step nothing appears over the video.
  3. Start the video, then search for a subtitle or drop a file you already have onto the picture.

Want something to test with? The sample video and subtitle files are timed and ready.

Some browser pages and video players restrict extensions, so compatibility can depend on the website.

Quick fixes.

The overlay doesn’t appear.

This is almost always site access. Click Subtitle Overlay in the toolbar and choose Always Allow on This Website, then reload the page. If it still fails, send the Safari version and the website or player name.

The subtitles are too early or too late.

Press , and . to shift the timing half a second at a time, or [ and ] for a tenth. Open the Sync Timeline at the right edge of the page to snap a specific line to the current moment. A subtitle release that matches your copy of the video usually needs less adjusting.

Subtitle search returns nothing.

Try a shorter title and check the language filter. Search uses OpenSubtitles and depends on its availability and daily limits. You can always load a subtitle file from your Mac instead.

The video is inside an embedded player and nothing happens.

Some players run in a separate frame. Click the toolbar button while that page is open and allow access, then reload. If it still does not work, tell us the site and we will look at it.

Which subtitle formats are supported?

SRT, SMI, VTT and ASS. Korean, Japanese and Chinese character encodings are detected automatically, so older files open without a mangled first line.

How do I stop sharing usage information?

Open the extension, go to Settings, General, and turn off usage data sharing. Sharing is off until you explicitly turn it on, and the app works exactly the same either way. See the privacy policy for what is collected and how to request deletion.
