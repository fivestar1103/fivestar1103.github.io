How to use them.

  1. Open Safari Settings, choose Extensions, and switch on Subtitle Overlay.
  2. Return to this page, click Subtitle Overlay in Safari's toolbar, and choose Always Allow on This Website. Without this step nothing will appear over the video, because Safari extensions start with no access to any page.
  3. Start the video above.
  4. Download demo-en.srt and drag it from Finder onto the video. Subtitles appear immediately.
  5. Press , or . to shift the timing, + or - to resize. Move the pointer to the right edge of the page to open the Sync Timeline, which lists every line and jumps to it.

Nothing here needs an account, a sign-in, or a payment. The subtitle file stays on your Mac.
